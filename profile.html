Public academic CV

Shi Shen 沈石

Lecturer, PhD · Beijing Normal University

My research develops theory, methods, and applications in geospatial data mining and GeoAI. I combine spatiotemporal modeling, deep learning, and geographic process models to study social sensing, human–environment systems, global environmental change, and disaster risk.
